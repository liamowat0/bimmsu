About

A professional student organization for healthcare-minded leaders.

  • Our Mission

    Medicine, Business, and Leadership

    Business in Medicine at Michigan State University provides a professional environment for students seeking experience at the intersection of medicine, healthcare, entrepreneurship, and business leadership.

  • Who We Serve

    MSU Students and Collaborative Participants

    Membership is designed for Michigan State University students, and participation in select activities may also be open to non-MSU students where appropriate.

  • Our Environment

    Interdisciplinary Exploration and Growth

    The organization connects future healthcare professionals with ambitions beyond solely clinical practice through discussion, collaboration, programming, and leadership development.

  • Future Pathways

    Preparation for Advanced Opportunities

    We help students prepare for future opportunities, including interdisciplinary trajectories such as MD/MBA pathways and other leadership-focused careers in healthcare.
