Executive Board

Student leaders guiding the direction and operations of the organization.

President

Provides strategic leadership, sets organizational priorities, and represents the club's mission across campus and professional settings.

Vice President

Supports organizational strategy, coordinates initiatives, and helps ensure effective execution of programming and member engagement.

Secretary

Maintains organizational records, documents key decisions, and supports communication to keep members informed and aligned.

Treasurer

Oversees budgeting and financial planning to sustain responsible operations and support mission-aligned activities.

The Executive Board may work alongside committees focused on programming, partnerships, recruitment, and event planning to advance student development.
